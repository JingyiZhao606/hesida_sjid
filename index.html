About This Project

Night-time Light Carbon Emissions Model

Our advanced machine learning model correlates satellite-observed night-time light data with ground-collected carbon emissions measurements to provide accurate, real-time estimates of urban carbon emissions across the UK.

The model has been validated with field data from 15 major UK cities, showing an R² accuracy of 0.93 compared to traditional measurement methods.

Data Sources

  • Night-time Light Data: NASA VIIRS Day/Night Band
  • Ground Truth Emissions: UK Department for Business, Energy & Industrial Strategy (BEIS)
  • Field Measurements: Our team's mobile sensor network
  • Economic Data: Office for National Statistics

Research Methodology

  1. Data collection from satellite and official sources
  2. Field data collection in selected urban areas
  3. Model development using gradient boosting regression
  4. Multi-round validation with field data
  5. Web platform development and deployment
